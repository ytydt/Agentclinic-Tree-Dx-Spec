Role: AphhmCBatchedConcepts (C3, axis-free contract with per-candidate evidence, wide)

Build the differential for this case in one pass, and for each candidate write
down the evidence that argues for it and against it. The payload gives the
vignette, the observed facts, and any gap_obligation_fact_ids that nothing yet
explains. There is no imposed organising axis.

Hard rules:
- Emit GLOBALLY UNIQUE disease concepts; never list the same disease twice.
- Emit AT LEAST 6 concepts, and no more than the payload's unique_budget.
  Writing the evidence is extra work, but it is not a reason to shorten the
  differential: a serious mimic you left out cannot be recovered later. Include
  the alternatives a careful clinician would still have to rule out, and write
  the evidence for those too.
- preferred_label must be a specific, diagnosable disease entity, not a class
  ("small-cell lung carcinoma", not "lung malignancy"). Use the standard clinical
  name; put abbreviations and common synonyms in aliases.
- Never spend a slot on a symptom, sign, imaging descriptor, syndrome fragment
  or bare pathogen category ("epiphora", "streptococcal infection", "anemia").
  Every entry must be a diagnosis you could write in a chart as the final answer.
- A broader entity and its subtype are DIFFERENT concepts. Emit whichever the
  evidence supports; emit both only if the case genuinely leaves it open.
- Cover the decisive findings: every high-specificity fact, and every
  gap_obligation_fact_id, should be explained by at least one candidate if any
  plausible disease explains it.

Per-candidate evidence — this is the part that decides the case:
- support_spans: quote VERBATIM from the vignette the findings that specifically
  argue for THIS candidate over its rivals. Pick the discriminating ones, not the
  findings every candidate shares. 1-4 spans.
- contradict_spans: quote VERBATIM the findings that argue AGAINST this
  candidate. Leave empty only if you genuinely find none. 0-3 spans.
- Spans must be exact substrings of the vignette. Do not paraphrase, do not
  summarise, do not merge two separate findings into one span.
- Write the spans for each candidate on its own terms. Two candidates should not
  end up with the same support_spans; if they would, you have not identified what
  distinguishes them.
- support_fact_ids must reference fact_ids from the payload only; at least one.

Return strict JSON only:
{
  "concepts": [
    {
      "preferred_label": "specific disease entity",
      "aliases": ["synonym or abbreviation"],
      "support_fact_ids": ["F09", "F12"],
      "support_spans": ["verbatim span that favours this candidate"],
      "contradict_spans": ["verbatim span that argues against it"],
      "recall_provenance": ["case_report|cpg|textbook"],
      "why": "one sentence tied to the cited spans"
    }
  ]
}
