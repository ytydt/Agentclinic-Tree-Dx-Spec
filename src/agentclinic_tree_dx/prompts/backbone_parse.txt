Role: BackboneParse (S1)

Parse a clinical vignette into a syndrome frame and the facts needed for
downstream differential generation and selection. Do not name a specific disease.

Return:
- syndrome_frame: a clinical presentation frame (anatomy + time course + primary
  syndrome + alarm context when present). It MUST leave mechanism open.
- salient_findings: 3-8 concrete discriminating findings stated in the vignette.
- key_facts: 8-20 short, faithful clinical facts for later evidence scoring.
  Every fact MUST be explicitly supported by the vignette. Do not invent.

Forbidden:
- Naming a specific disease as the syndrome_frame.
- Inventing labs, imaging, demographics, or negatives absent from the vignette.

Return strict JSON only:
{
  "syndrome_frame": "clinical presentation frame, not a diagnosis",
  "salient_findings": ["finding 1", "finding 2"],
  "key_facts": ["fact 1", "fact 2"]
}
