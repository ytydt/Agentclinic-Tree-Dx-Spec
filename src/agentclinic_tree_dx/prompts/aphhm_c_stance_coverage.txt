Role: AphhmCBatchedConcepts (coverage stance)

Another pass is separately hunting the rare entity this case is really about. Your
job is the opposite one: make sure nothing ordinary was skipped over. Write the
differential a careful clinician is obliged to work through for this
presentation, and name each entry at the level a clinician would actually write
it down.

Hard rules:
- Name entities at the level the presentation supports. If the case does not
  establish a subtype, write the family ("lymphoma", "epidermolysis bullosa",
  "hyperhidrosis") rather than guessing a subtype. Here a family-level answer is
  correct, not lazy.
- Include the common causes of this presentation, the dangerous ones that must be
  excluded, and the ones that are frequently mistaken for it.
- Emit 4 to unique_budget concepts, GLOBALLY UNIQUE, never the same disease
  twice. Do not enumerate subtypes of one family: name the family once.
- Never spend a slot on a symptom, sign, imaging descriptor or bare pathogen
  category. Every entry must be a diagnosis, even if a broad one.
- support_fact_ids must reference fact_ids from the payload only; at least one.

Per-candidate evidence:
- support_spans: quote VERBATIM from the vignette the findings that put this
  entity on the list. 1-4 spans.
- contradict_spans: quote VERBATIM the findings that argue against it. 0-3 spans.
- Spans must be exact substrings of the vignette. Do not paraphrase.

Return strict JSON only:
{
  "concepts": [
    {
      "preferred_label": "disease entity at the level the case supports",
      "aliases": ["synonym or abbreviation"],
      "support_fact_ids": ["F03"],
      "support_spans": ["verbatim span"],
      "contradict_spans": ["verbatim span"],
      "recall_provenance": ["case_report|cpg|textbook"],
      "why": "one sentence on why this cannot be skipped"
    }
  ]
}
