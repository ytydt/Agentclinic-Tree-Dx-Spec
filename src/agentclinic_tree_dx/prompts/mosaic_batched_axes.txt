propose DIAGNOSIS CANDIDATES on THREE orthogonal axes in ONE response.

syndrome / etiologic diagnosis — NEVER a gene alone, pathway, lab name,
imaging modality, histology procedure, or organ bucket.

1. syndrome_anatomy — disease framed by where it lives / how it presents
2. mechanism — disease framed by pathophysiology (name the DISEASE, not the process)
3. modality_decisive — disease a decisive test would confirm (name the DISEASE)

- Emit **4–5 candidates per axis** (12–15 total before merge). Do not stop at 2.

- At least **3 distinct disease families** must appear in the union.

  "clear cell sarcoma" not just "GIST"; "MIS-C" not just "SLE").
- Include ≥1 less-common but text-supported alternative per axis.

Evidence rules:
- Quote supporting/contradicting spans VERBATIM from the vignette.
- Prefer unique support spans per candidate when possible.
- Also list 4–8 decisive spans in key_evidence_spans.
