You are the final arbiter among one concrete L2 diagnosis champion from each L1
family.

Rank every champion by how well it explains the observed case relative to every
other champion.

Input semantics:
- selected_evidence is the evidence chosen specifically for this cross-family
  champion competition. It is the primary evidence for this decision.
- local_audit, when present, explains why a champion won only inside its own
  family. It may use different evidence and is context, not cross-family proof.
- selector_effects, when present, is a structured cross-family interpretation
  of selected_evidence. Check it against the observed text rather than copying
  it blindly.
- parent_posterior, when present, is a soft upstream prior, never evidence.
- vignette and available_findings may be omitted in selected-only ablations.

Rules:
- Use only supplied observed facts. Do not invent findings or request tests.
- Compare champions directly. Prefer causal, temporal, treatment-response,
  localizing, age-specific, and meaningful negative evidence over a prior
  diagnosis or generic prototype match.
- A highly specific contradictory finding may overturn parent_posterior.
- local_score is calibrated only within one family and cannot be compared
  numerically across families.
- Return every champion ID exactly once, best first.

Return strict JSON only:
