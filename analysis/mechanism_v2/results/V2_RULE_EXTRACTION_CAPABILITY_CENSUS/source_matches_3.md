The most informative paired traces:

- **S3-02, EEG non-exclusion.** Both arms put `relation=excludes` and `polarity=negated` on the predicate `normal EEG`. The source prohibits the exclusion inference; the schema’s polarity negates a feature. Those are different logical operators. The absence of relation-level NOT/test-non-exclusion representation is a schema limit, while the particular malformed encoding is already visible in raw output. The extra `supports` relation is intelligible but outside the enum; schema illegality is recorded separately. New additionally promotes a source workup condition—suspected structural cause should trigger imaging—to `structural cause required_for Epilepsy`; this is a traceable non-target-to-diagnostic distortion outside the two frozen target units.
- **S3-03, bacteremia focus contamination.** Both arms replace gram-negative bacteremia with focus `Gram-negative bacillary infection`. The conditional skin-with-decubitus-ulcers branch becomes independent skin/ulcer atoms in old and unqualified skin origin in new. New also turns bacteremia causing endocarditis into focus infection `caused_by endocarditis`. None requires a missing source table to explain the mismatch.
- **S3-06, mixed correct/wrong marker descendants.** S100 correctly supports nerve-sheath tumor in both calls, but the same source quotation also produces negated-S100 `excludes` fibrosarcoma. Likewise, correct weak vascular-marker associations coexist with wrong-polarity rigid exclusions of fibrosarcoma. The ledger keeps all recognizable descendants; a correct atom does not erase a conflicting companion.
- **S3-07, taxonomy coverage regresses.** Old retains incomplete causal heads for PH groups 2–5; new omits these entirely while recovering responder/nonresponder words for IPAH. The latter become typical features instead of conditioned response subclasses. This table is a classification hierarchy, not a true diagnostic all-of panel; do not use its nested structure as evidence of recovered operational criteria.
- **S3-08, AFX exclusion diagnosis versus negative stains.** Both calls recover six stain names but encode AFX-negative staining as `excludes + negated`, and turn sparse S100 into categorical absence. Both also reduce the whole histologic exclusion requirement to comparator disease names or the word immunohistochemistry. Old/new negative Ras mutations similarly become an exclusion rule while losing the single-study scope. By contrast, the complete positive nonspecific-stain list is faithfully retained as weak, ungrouped `feature_of` atoms: no fabricated AND is required for this descriptive set.
- **S3-10, member recall without whole-rule recovery.** New adds the four possible KS sites that old omitted; the full distribution/optionality is still incomplete. Both preserve melanoma size 2–6 cm but detach it from its oral-cavity scope. Both omit palatal NHL’s junction location and lack of ulceration. These are partial source descendants, not whole omissions or source-free fabrications.
- **S3-13, more text but worse entity relation.** Old keeps only the noun “classification” from MFH reclassification. New copies all four alternative labels into its predicate, then labels the relation `synonym_of`. Thus recovering more source words creates a wrong equivalence relation, while the source’s differentiation/genetics-conditioned reclassification remains missing.
- **S3-14, changed grouping with definite scope/coverage loss.** Old `g1=any` contains low-grade fever, leukocytosis, localized pain and optional radicular radiation. New `g1=all` contains the three main features and drops radiation. All members remain `feature_of/typical`: an `all` group can describe a soft joint pattern and does **not by itself** establish a hard disease necessity or exclusion rule. The confirmed whole-source distortions are missing pain provocation by percussion/pressure in both calls, loss of optional modality in old, and omission of the radiation member in new. Missing explicit group effect is separately a contract warning. New also converts missed-diagnosis paraplegia prognosis into negated-paraplegia `argues_against` epidural abscess, a separate traceable task/effect error.
- **S3-15, epistemic scope is not literal polarity.** Both arms change “blood transmission is not known” into an exclusion with negated blood-transmission predicate. Old attaches “theoretical” to the vCJD transfusion route; the source attaches it to transplant-recipient risk. New uses a legal modality (`typical`/`occasional`) but does not repair the epistemic meaning. A valid enum cannot certify faithful semantics.

The frozen zero-rule windows are useful negative controls. S3-09 still emits 8/6 assertions and S3-12 emits 6/9. Their existence does **not** imply source-free hallucination: the former has visible referral/treatment/prognosis ancestors, while the latter has pathophysiologic ancestors. In S3-09 new, “a prompt biopsy should be considered” becomes biopsy `required_for` cutaneous vascular malignancy, demonstrating diagnostic-strength promotion from workup prose. These extra outputs belong to the separate output-side denominator; they are not added retrospectively to the source inventory.

Evidence discipline:

- `first_damage=raw_output` means the complete supplied source and historical raw cache already demonstrate the mismatch/omission before downstream normalization; this localization has B-level direct-cache evidence. It does not prove a unique root cause.
- C-level prompt/model explanations are kept non-exclusive. Both calls are historical samples; an observed pair is not by itself a fully controlled model-capacity experiment.
- A-level schema observations cite the actual fields. Group-level effect, nested child scope and relation-level negation are absent. The broad split-every-“and” instruction is present, but its existence alone does not prove that homogeneous soft groups distort their descriptive source; a downstream hard-veto interpretation would be a separately localized execution error.
- Invalid enums, comparator-contract violations and nonverbatim quotes are in `schema_errors`. They do not independently force distortion or justify “untraceable fabrication.” Correct source ancestry can survive a bad quote.
- This is an AI audit, not a clinically adjudicated independent gold standard. The pack’s complete faithful units, ambiguous cases and segmentation-sensitive taxonomy should be included in second review.
