Patient-Friendly, Plain-Language Recommendations 
The ACR/VF Clinical Practice Guidelines were written for medical practitioners to help guide them in their treatment of patients with vasculitis. The plain-language versions of these guidelines focus on the information that is most important for the majority of patients to know and are written in language that can be easily understood by those who do not have a medical background. Find the patient-friendly, plain-language recommendations on the Vasculitis Foundation website (select the Learn More box under each guideline title).
