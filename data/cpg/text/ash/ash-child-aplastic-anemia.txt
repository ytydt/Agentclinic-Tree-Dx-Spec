ASH Clinical Practice Guidelines on Aplastic Anemia 
Over recent years, there has been an increase in available information, knowledge, and expertise in aplastic anemia. The purpose of these guidelines is to provide evidence-based recommendations about the diagnosis and management of severe and very severe acquired aplastic anemia.
