Implementation 
Through engagement with stakeholders and with the help of the guideline committee, NICE has identified areas of its guideline on menopause that (at the time of the 2024 update) may be a significant change to practice or challenging to implement. These are: 
access to healthcare professionals with expertise in menopause 
communicating the benefits and risks of hormone replacement therapy (HRT) 
access to menopause specific cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) 
equity of services . 
For further information and support, see tools and resources .
