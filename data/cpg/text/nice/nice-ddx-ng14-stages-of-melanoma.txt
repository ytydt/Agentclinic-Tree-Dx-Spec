Stages of melanoma 
The stages of melanoma referred to in this guideline are based on the 8th editions of the Union for International Cancer Control (UICC) tumour node metastasis (TNM) classification of malignant tumours and the American Joint Committee on Cancer (AJCC) melanoma staging system.
