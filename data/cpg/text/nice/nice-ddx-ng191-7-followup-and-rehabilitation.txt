7 Follow‑up and rehabilitation 
For follow-up and rehabilitation for people who have either ongoing symptomatic COVID-19 or post-COVID-19 syndrome, see NICE's guideline on the long-term effects of COVID-19 . 
Be aware of the UK Government's information on the COVID-19 vaccination programme .
