## Evaluation

Evaluation 
 CBC
 NG lavage
 Consider for suspected sig. GI blood loss
 Small child: 12F NG tube; instill 50cc saline
 Older child: 14-16F NG tube; instill 100-200cc saline
 Aspirate after 2-3min
