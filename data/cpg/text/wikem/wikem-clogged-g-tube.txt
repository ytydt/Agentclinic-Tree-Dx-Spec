## Clinical Features

Clinical Features 
 Child with percutaneous gastrostomy tube (G-tube)
 G-tube can be clogged with medications or feeding solution
 Inability to pass feeds or aspirate from g-tube


## Differential Diagnosis

Differential Diagnosis


## G-tube complications

G-tube complications 
 Clogged G-tube
 Displaced G-tube
 Infected G-tube
 Leaking G-tube


## Evaluation

Evaluation 
 Clinical diagnosis
 Attempts should not be made to clear tube if uncertain position/integrity of tube


## Disposition

Disposition 
 Discharge if:
 G-tube successfully unclogged and functioning
 Patient tolerating feeds
 Caregiver able to manage at home

 Admit for:
 Unable to unclog tube and requires replacement
 Signs of peritonitis or tube dislodgement with peritoneal contamination
 Dehydration or electrolyte abnormalities from prolonged inability to feed
