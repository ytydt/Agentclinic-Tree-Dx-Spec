For more than 75 years, the ACC has been a trusted source of evidence-based cardiovascular guidance, delivering timely, actionable recommendations that support clinicians in providing optimal patient care. Through its close partnership with JACC Journals , the College ensures that this guidance and related resources are rigorously developed, widely disseminated and readily accessible at the point of care.
