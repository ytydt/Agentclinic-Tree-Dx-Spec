ASH VTE Guidelines for Latin America Adaptations

ASH partnered with 12 societies to adapt the ASH VTE Clinical Practice Guidelines for Latin America. The panel selected questions from the original ASH VTE guidelines that are clinically significant in Latin America and reviewed the evidence to form recommendations.

Access the full guidelines on the Blood Advances website:

ASH, ABHH, ACHO, Grupo CAHT, Grupo CLAHT, SAH, SBHH, SHU, SOCHIHEM, SOMETH, Sociedad Panameña de Hematología, SPH, SVH, 2022 Guidelines for diagnosis of Venous Thromboembolism and management in Special Populations in Latin America

ASH, ABHH, ACHO, Grupo CAHT, Grupo CLAHT, SAH, SBHH, SHU, SOCHIHEM, SOMETH, Sociedad Panameña de Hematología, SPH, SVH, 2022 Guidelines for Prevention of Venous Thromboembolism in Surgical and Medical Patients, and Long-Distance Travelers in Latin America

ASH, ABHH, ACHO, Grupo CAHT, Grupo  CLAHT, SAH, SBHH, SHU, SOCHIHEM, SOMETH, Sociedad Panameña de Hematología, SPH, and SVH 2021 guidelines for management of venous thromboembolism in Latin America

ASH Clinical Practice Guidelines App

The ASH Clinical Practice Guidelines App provides easy access to every recommendation from all guidelines published by ASH, including rationale for each recommendation, benefits and harms associated with each recommended course of action, and links to the complete evidence-to-decision tables used to develop the recommendations. This app is also available via web interface.

Download for iOS  |  Download for Android

La App sobre Prácticas Clínicas de la ASH proporciona fácil acceso a cada recomendación de todas las guías publicadas por la ASH, inclusive la razón fundamental para cada recomendación, beneficios y problemas asociados a cada serie de acciones recomendadas, además de enlaces a cuadros completos sobre evidencia-para-decisión usados para el establecimiento de las recomendaciones. Esta app también está disponible via web interface.
