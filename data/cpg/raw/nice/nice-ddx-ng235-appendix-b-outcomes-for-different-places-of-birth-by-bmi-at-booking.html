The data in these tables are based on a population of women of mixed ethnicity. No data was available for different ethnic groups. New reference ranges defining overweight and obesity for non-pregnant women from different ethnic groups are available in the NICE guideline on overweight and obesity management, but the correlation of these revised ranges with intrapartum risks for women and their babies is not known.
