Women’s health guidance for family physicians

Women’s health care rarely fits neatly into a single system or specialty and involves balancing immediate concerns with long-term health planning. Family physicians hold all those threads at once.

Continuity is what makes this work possible. Seeing patients over time helps distinguish what is temporary, what is progressive and where risk may be quietly accumulating.

Reproductive care, pregnancy and health beyond the reproductive years each bring different prevention priorities, risk considerations and counseling needs. The areas below organize clinical guidance around those phases of care, helping you match today’s visit with the life context shaping a patient’s health.
